03 / Leadership & engineering

AlbertaSat ASCENT — Power Team Lead.

I lead the Power Team for ASCENT, AlbertaSat’s high-altitude balloon mission, as part of an interdisciplinary team of around 20 members. The power subsystem has to provide, regulate, monitor, and distribute electrical power across the onboard computer, tracking, communications, sensors, and scientific payload.

My current work is focused on power-distribution PCB design, voltage regulation, overcurrent protection, and embedded power monitoring. I’m designing for a mission environment that can reach about −60°C and 1.2 kPa, while keeping the system reliable through changing operational loads.
